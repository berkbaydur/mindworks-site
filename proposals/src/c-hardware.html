    05 — Company

    An independent technology consultancy, founded in London in 2021.

    Mindworks Technology Ltd was founded in April 2021 to give technology companies direct access to senior engineering across software development, connectivity, device management and DevOps.

    Its clients build products where software meets hardware: industrial networking equipment, connected home appliances, and the platforms and infrastructure behind them. Engagements range from a few days of architectural advice to long-running platform delivery and operations.
