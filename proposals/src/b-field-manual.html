§ 01ServicesContents

Six ways to work with Mindworks.

Clients combine whichever ones the work needs.

  1. System design reviews, target architectures and the decisions in between: build or buy, monolith or services, which cloud, which protocol. Clients receive written recommendations their teams can act on, with the reasoning behind each one.

    • Architecture reviews
    • Tech due diligence
    • ADRs
    • Roadmaps

  2. Connected products from the device up: provisioning, telemetry pipelines, OTA updates, fleet management and the dashboards on top. This is the layer where hardware and software teams most often talk past each other, and where Mindworks does its best work.

    • Device management
    • Fleet tracking
    • 4G/LTE
    • MQTT
    • Remote updates

  3. When a project needs code more than advice, Mindworks builds it: backend services, APIs, integrations and the infrastructure they run on, delivered with tests, documentation and a codebase the client team can own.

    • Backend and APIs
    • Cloud infrastructure
    • CI/CD
    • Integrations

  4. Keeping production fast, stable and observable. Mindworks manages servers and cloud infrastructure, configures CDNs and traffic routing, and sets up monitoring and alerting so issues are caught before customers notice. Available as a one-off hardening project or as ongoing support.

    • Server management
    • CDN
    • Load balancing
    • Monitoring
    • Alerting

  5. Engineering leadership while a company hires, reorganises or recovers a delivery that has slipped. That covers running the team day to day, introducing processes that fit its size, and a clean handover once a permanent lead is in place.

    • Engineering management
    • Delivery process
    • Hiring
    • Mentoring

  6. Before a client commits to a platform, vendor or tool, Mindworks trials it against their real constraints, reports the findings and, if it holds up, leads the rollout. Problems surface in week one rather than month six.

    • Vendor trials
    • Proofs of concept
    • Rollout
    • Developer tooling

Case 01Industrial IoT, implemented and maintained

Tens of thousandsof industrial routers tracked and managed.

Device management platform for industrial 4G routers

A manufacturer of industrial 4G routers needed visibility and control over devices deployed in the field. Mindworks designed and implemented the platform that tracks and manages that router fleet, and continues to maintain and operate it.

Its clients build products where software meets hardware: industrial networking equipment, connected home appliances, and the platforms and infrastructure behind them. Engagements range from a few days of architectural advice to long-running platform delivery and operations.
