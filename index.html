§03 Engagements

Billed by the hour. Judged by what ships.

  1. Introductory call

    A free thirty-minute conversation to understand the problem and confirm whether Mindworks is the right fit.

  2. Scope & rate

    A short written scope, an agreed hourly rate and an estimate of hours. Retainers are available but never required.

  3. Work in the open

    Mindworks works inside the client's own tools, repositories and rituals. Hours are logged against tasks, so every invoice is fully traceable.

  4. Hand over

    Documentation, recorded walkthroughs and a team that can carry on independently. That is how Mindworks measures a successful engagement.

§05 Company

An independent technology consultancy, founded in London in 2021.

Mindworks Technology Ltd was founded in April 2021 to give technology companies direct access to senior engineering across software development, connectivity, device management and DevOps.

Its clients build products where software meets hardware: industrial networking equipment, connected home appliances, and the platforms and infrastructure behind them. Engagements range from a few days of architectural advice to long-running platform delivery and operations. Every engagement is scoped in writing and billed transparently by the hour.
